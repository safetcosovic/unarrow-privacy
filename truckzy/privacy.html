Truckzy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. App: Truckzy: Truck Parking Puzzle (Android package and iOS bundle ID com.truckzy.cargo).

Summary

Truckzy is an offline puzzle game. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, user accounts or analytics of our own, and we do not sell your personal data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, coins, boosters, the daily gift calendar and your settings are stored locally on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is protected with an integrity check so that it cannot be edited by other apps.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a full-screen ad after every 10 completed levels and when you run out of hearts, a banner ad on Easy levels, and optional rewarded videos only when you choose to watch one (for an extra loading dock or a Crane or Sort booster). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a third party. To show, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interaction data, and app diagnostic and performance data. Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. On iOS you are asked for permission (App Tracking Transparency) before the advertising identifier is used for tracking. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Residents of US states with privacy laws can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information through the same Privacy options or the device's ad settings.

Children

Truckzy is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are requested in a way that is suitable for a general audience.

Deleting your data

Delete all game data with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing else for us to delete. For data processed by Google, use Google's controls at myaccount.google.com.

Security

The app does not transmit game data. Ad traffic is handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The app requests only the Internet and network-state permissions needed for ads (and, on Android 13+, the advertising ID permission).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
