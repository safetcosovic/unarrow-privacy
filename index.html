Unarrow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · App: Unarrow (Android package com.unarrow.puzzle)

Summary

Unarrow is a puzzle game. It stores your game progress and settings only on your device. We do not run servers, user accounts or analytics of our own, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data ourselves. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, achievements and settings (sound, theme and similar) are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows:

  • a full-screen ad after every 10 completed levels and after you lose all three hearts;
  • a banner ad on Easy levels;
  • optional rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one (to get a hint or to remove three arrows).

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a third party. To show, measure and protect ads, AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interaction data, and app diagnostic and performance data. This data is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the EU, EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. On iOS you are asked for permission before your device's advertising identifier is used for tracking. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

Unarrow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Deleting your data

You can delete all game data with Settings → Reset progress in the app, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. For data processed by Google AdMob, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

Security

Ad data is transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
