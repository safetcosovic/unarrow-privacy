ZOOMBLE – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · App: ZOOMBLE – Kart Knockout Royale (iOS bundle com.zoomble.Zoomble, Android package com.zoomble.kartroyale)

Summary

ZOOMBLE is a kart racing game. It stores your game progress and settings only on your device. We do not run servers, user accounts, chat or analytics of our own, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data ourselves. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Coins, gems, crowns, XP and level, unlocked karts, colours and hats, Tour progress, daily-reward streak and settings (sound, kart speed, camera distance, graphics) are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows:

  • a banner ad on the title screen and in the lobby;
  • a full-screen ad when you return to the menu after a finished match (not during your first three matches, and at most once every 3 minutes);
  • optional rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one (to double the coins earned in a match or the daily reward, or to receive a few free gems, up to three times a day).

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a third party. To show, measure and protect ads, AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID / IDFA), IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interaction data, and app diagnostic and performance data. This data is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the EU, EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. On iOS you are asked for permission (App Tracking Transparency) before your device's advertising identifier is used for tracking; if you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Sharing and ratings

After a win you can tap “Share” to open your device's own share menu with a short message and a link to the game. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself, and we do not receive what you share. On iOS the game may once ask Apple's standard “rate this app” prompt; your rating goes to Apple, not to us.

Children

ZOOMBLE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Deleting your data

All game data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. For data processed by Google AdMob, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

Security

Ad data is transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
