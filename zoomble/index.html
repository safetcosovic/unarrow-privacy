ZOOMBLE – Kart Knockout Royale

Grab the wheel and survive the chaos! ZOOMBLE is a party kart racer where every round knocks out the slowest drivers until only one is crowned. Race through Sunny Meadows, Candy Canyon, Frosty Peaks, Dusty Dunes, Lava Rumble and Sunset Bay, battle with items, and outlast everyone on the collapsing hex arena.

Support

Questions, bugs or feedback: safet.cosovic@gmail.com. Please include your device model and what happened — we usually reply within 2 business days.

FAQ

Do I need an internet connection? No, you can play offline. Ads need a connection.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The karts are too fast! Open Settings and set Kart speed to SLOW. You can also move the camera further back there.
